Server reports with reasons and a personal blocked list. Moderator, Official Tester CZM and Server owner roles with styled profiles. In-game moderation requires reasons, records actions, pauses after 10 unreviewed actions and lets the head administrator undo them. Testers can send a ticket with a photo and text file. Project owners can list up to five servers, joinable from their profile. An optional Telegram contact is available only to ordinary accounts.
